## How the additions were found

Web searches for biography wording around unnamed relatives and teachers found these candidate pages. This is useful for discovery, but it currently over-samples family relations. Batch 006 adds one organization target through a named work; batch 007 adds two historical parent relations; batch 008 adds two unknown-birth-parent cases, one highly contested; batch 009 adds two more unknown-father cases with adoptive-family context; batch 010 broadens beyond biographies to an artwork and four papyrus artifacts with unknown creators. This is not a representative benchmark sample. Do not use the search hit rate as a prevalence estimate.

## Next steps after the first-pass screen

1. Resolve the 43 unresolved rows, starting with weak-source and disputed claims. Reject any row that fails source/context verification.
2. Agree how to handle multiple unnamed targets, notnames, deliberate pseudonyms, proposed identities, and people identified elsewhere in article context.
3. Verify the source and licence for every retained row; record page revision and capture relevant Wikidata claims in a dated evidence slice.
4. Recount the validated set after exclusions. If fewer than 100 remain, collect genuine replacement examples rather than padding the pool.
5. Keep gold labels blank until annotation; then use a second annotator and adjudicate disagreements before freezing a split.
6. Separately run a standard Text2KGBench no-harm check. This candidate pool supports the anonymous-node problem; it does not replace standard benchmark evaluation.
